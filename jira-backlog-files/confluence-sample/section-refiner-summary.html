What the columns mean

  • Not pointed: the story has no points.
  • Description needs update: the description is empty, 20 characters or fewer, or not written as "As a ..., I want ..., so that ...".
  • Acceptance criteria needs update: the acceptance criteria are empty, 20 characters or fewer, or only vague words.
  • Suggest split: the story looks bigger than its points: 5 points or more with little written, or 3 points or fewer for a kind of work that usually takes longer.
  • Missing info: something required is empty (and the story is not a split suggestion).
  • Needs review: something is short, vague or off, but nothing is missing.
  • Looks good: no findings.

Each story is counted once per row. The first three measure columns can overlap (a story can be both unpointed and missing a description). The last four are each story's single verdict, so they add up to the number of stories.

This page shows counts only. It does not name people or stories.
